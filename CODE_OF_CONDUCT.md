# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

AI Mastery Roadmap should be a constructive learning and engineering environment.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- critique ideas, code, and decisions rather than people;
- explain disagreements with enough context to be useful;
- welcome questions asked in good faith;
- avoid gatekeeping based on experience level, education, background, or career path;
- give credit for ideas, code, and source material where appropriate;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or confidential information without permission.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate humiliation, threats, doxxing, spam, or sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Learning-Specific Expectations

This project values demonstrated understanding over appearance.

It is acceptable to make mistakes, ask basic questions, revise earlier work, or document failed approaches. It is not acceptable to misrepresent generated, copied, or externally authored work as independently understood work when that distinction matters to a learning task.

Feedback should help improve understanding rather than merely point out that something is wrong.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces associated with AI Mastery Roadmap.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these standards. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project participation.

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private contact channel rather than by publishing sensitive details in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, and the safety of participants.
